# C3 Algorithm and error contract

Sort positive low atoms by increasing energy/key and positive high atoms by decreasing energy/key. A complete high-energy-level sweep accumulates its tail B and eligible low prefix A to obtain delta. A second monotone residual sweep constructs low-high edges. Finish with low diagonal halves and high singles. At most n_positive maximum event records are emitted. An intermediate target uses at most2n_positive records. The optional selected witness never replaces the maximum witness used to certify optimality.

The verifier recalculates each atom marginal and pair energy, threshold deficit, objective, sparse bound and input identity. Its three dual-value groups cover every possible allowed pair/loop through the minimum energy in each relevant group. The independent verifier uses a different path: sorted energies plus arbitrary-dual eligible-prefix minima. The generic small oracle explicitly builds every pair/loop and enumerates up to3^7 assignments; it imports no production solver and is used only on the48 frozen small cases.

All new core arithmetic is exact Fraction/int arithmetic. There is no numerical tolerance, iterative convergence threshold, sampling error estimate or floating residual admission. The original saved binary64 source values are lifted exactly; their prior physical/model/discretization uncertainty is not thereby eliminated. Rounding is used only for human display and plotted coordinates, never acceptance. O(nlogn) comparison/sorting and O(n) sweep arithmetic refer to operation counts; integer/rational bit complexity is not constant.

The production problem has3201 saved atoms. It does not materialize the O(n²) atom graph, infer unrecorded electron support, or search a physical energy cap. Nine predetermined cap samples are conditional cases, not a scan that infers actual K. Plotted sample points do not certify a continuous interpolation. Distinct decimal and stored-cutoff thresholds are evaluated exactly even when they coincide visually on a floating display axis.

Root runs the fixed finite primary, negative controls, independent reference and saved-result plot under the separate measured budget. Any actual failure remains in the ledger. Further tests are allowed only to resolve a concrete remaining finding within the same remaining allowance; no generic repeated sweep or new scope is selected after sufficient verification.
