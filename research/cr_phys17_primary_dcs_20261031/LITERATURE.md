## What the user still needs to provide (if available)

Both originally requested 1976 PDF files are **now supplied**, no need to provide them again. The **next distinct new data** needed for stronger 2p uniqueness are (a) E~14.6–21eV 2p-resolved **CCC/ECS partial-wave T-matrix or complex magnetic-sublevel amplitudes**, including exchange/spin, normalization, and incident-energy/angle grids; (b) collisional H 2p magnetic sublevel density matrix / electron-photon angular-correlation parameter under same energy, polarized or angle-averaged; (c) full RII microscopic detailed-balance kernel and evolving neutral+geometry history to couple the radiation owner. Search APS/PRA DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.46.6995 for methodology and contact corresponding data holders for below-21eV arrays; do not treat 35eV tables as threshold data. Hummer/Rybicki original equations via DOI. Source absolute 2p integral is separately bound by NIST BE model and must not be silently replaced.
