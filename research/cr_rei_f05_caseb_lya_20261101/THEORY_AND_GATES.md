## R4/R5 constructive cascade physics and original source crosswalk
An individual Case-B recombination to levels n≥2 ultimately cascades to ground; if it takes the 2p→1s channel, **one** Lyα photon is produced. If 2s→1s, there is no Lyα and the energy is carried by two photons. Thus define the *emitted-recombination-Lyα sector only*:
`R_Lya,rec,j = P_B,j R_HB,j`, where `0 ≤ P_B,j ≤ 1` and the physically robust source bound is `0 ≤ R_Lya,rec,j ≤ R_HB,j`. Additional Lyα channels (collisional excitation, fluorescence, etc.) are excluded; the bound is NOT for total Lyα emission. All line photons can be reabsorbed; it is neither escape rate nor observed line luminosity.
A source-conditional temperature-dependent example uses Dijkstra/Cantalupo Case-B fit, interpreted in standard astrophysical `log=log10`: `P_B^fit(T)=0.686−0.106 log10(T/10^4 K)−0.009(T/10^4 K)^(−0.44)`. Its uncertainty is not quantified solely by source interval widths. `E_α = h c/λ_α` with λ_α=1215.6701 Å, h=6.62607015e−27 erg·s and c=2.99792458e10 cm/s. `L_Lya,rec^fit=Eα Σ_j P_B^fit(T_j)R_HB,j` erg/s is a conditional **intrinsic emissivity candidate**, no transport, no heating claim.
