## R4–R5 | Exact source-point physics and a two-axis CR materiality frontier

For each original source cell at a fixed accepted epoch:
`dot N_HII^0 = I_H^0-R_H^0`, `R_H^0>=0`. HII creation `I_H^0` INCLUDES original direct H photo/collisional ionization `I_H,dir` and **He-mediated OTS positive H ionizations** `I_H,OTS`; do not identify native `HI_ION` nine-event rate alone with the full HII creation rate. Original nine-event published sums give `R_H^0=sum_j HII_CASE_B_j`, `I_H,dir=sum_j HI_ION_j`. Our actual original 184,320-row source gives `S_H=sum_j dot N_HII,j`. Therefore independently identifiable **source-total** `I_H^tot = S_H+R_H`, `I_H,OTS=I_H^tot-I_H,dir`. Positive He-OTS residual must be preserved, not absorbed into hypothetical CR.

At fixed material state, introduce a hypothetical **effective** extra CR H ionization hazard `zeta_CR,eff >= 0` [s^-1 per HI], inclusive of relevant secondary H ionizations but not assumed to follow a specified CR proton flux. With `A=sum_j N_HI,j >0`, additional source `delta dot N_HII=zeta A`, `delta dot N_e=zeta A`, `delta dot B=chi_H zeta A` [erg/s after eV conversion], and independently `delta dot U=epsilon_heat zeta A` with `epsilon_heat>=0` [erg per net H ion]. The epsilon is gas TOTAL internal energy actually deposited **after** CR excitation, secondaries, escaping photons and chemical binding, not a source-owned measured yield. `B` and `U` remain separate ledgers; do not add binding energy to gas heat.

For fixed-source **global extensive** HII and total gas U:
- Original `S_H<0` is the original net source recombination (HII declining), `P_U=sum_j dot U_j<0` is native original total gas cooling.
- The exact affine *source-point* chemical zero-net threshold is `zeta_chem=-S_H/A>0` [s^-1]. It yields zero GLOBAL source-point net HII rate if such a new source were added (not an actual rerun or local sign reversal).
- The exactly defined native *direct H source matching threshold* is `zeta_Hdir=I_H,dir/A`, and **total** H creation source matching is `zeta_Htotal=I_H^tot/A`. If `zeta_Hdir < zeta_Htotal`, He-OTS amplifies the original baseline and comparison to direct H alone underestimates CR required source magnitude.
- The total gas energy zero-net threshold is the **two-dimensional frontier** `zeta_CR epsilon_heat=-P_U/A` [erg s^-1 per HI]. Neither epsilon nor zeta is measured by the original source. At the original H chemical source threshold, the required heat per extra ion is parameter-free `epsilon_star=(-P_U)/(-S_H)` [erg/ion], or `epsilon_star_eV=epsilon_star/(1.602176634e-12)`. Thus `zeta=zeta_chem` and `epsilon=epsilon_star` satisfy both GLOBAL instantaneous source equalities.

The gas temperature response cannot be equated to `delta dot U` because one additional ionization adds a free electron to the gas:
`U=(3/2)k_B T Npart`, `Npart=N_H+N_He+N_e`,
`delta dot ln T = zeta N_HI( epsilon_heat/U - 1/Npart)`.
The *local* critical heat per extra H ion that leaves source-only temperature unaffected is `epsilon_T,j=(3/2) k_B T_j`, directly source-defined with no CR spectrum. For epsilon below this, composition dilution can lower T even when CR total internal energy is injected. Wolfram independently simplifies the difference of expressions to **0** and derives `epsilon_T=(3/2)k_B T`; it also checks `zeta_chem epsilon_star A + P_U=0`. Do not mistake this for actual temperature evolution or a bound on nonlinear feedback.
